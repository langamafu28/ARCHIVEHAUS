OUR MISSION AT ARCHIVE HAUS:

Our mission is to make second-hand fashion affordable, accessible, and exciting by giving pre-loved pieces a brand-new life. We strive to make fashion fun and easy through a seamless online shopping experience.

OUR VISION AT ARCHIVE HAUS:

Our vision is to build the world's most trusted and reliable digital space for sustainable and unique fashion and inspire a global community that values people, the planet and pre-loved clothing.
