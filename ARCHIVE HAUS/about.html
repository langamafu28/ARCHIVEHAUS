ABOUT US

Welcome to ARCHIVE HAUS.

Where one of one pieces are just a tap away.

Archive Haus is a South African online thrift shop.

It was founded by award–winning stylist Langa Mafu in 2021. Archive Haus was created with the intention of giving vintage designer pieces a brand-new life whilst catering to the hearts of people who love vintage designer pieces at affordable prices.

Archive Haus has partnered up with some of the biggest fashion houses like - Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Gucci, Miu Miu, Jacqueemus and many more - to give shoppers a variety of designer pieces to choose from.

Thank you for being apart of our journey. This is more than clothing. Its about sustainability and conscious consumption
